04 · Motion Principles

Motion That Earns Attention

All entrance animations use opacity + translateY only. Scale, rotate, bounce, and spring are explicitly forbidden. Motion should feel like something arriving, not performing.

Easing
cubic-bezier
(0.25, 0.46, 0.45, 0.94)
Applied to every transition on the site. A gentle deceleration that signals confidence — nothing snaps, nothing bounces.
Duration Range
400ms – 700ms
Fast: 400ms for nav/hover states. Base: 550–600ms for panel transitions. Slow: 700ms for headline entrances.
Stagger Interval
80–120ms
Used for sequential element entrances — the IAM logo letters stagger at 90ms each. Cards stagger from first to last at 80ms intervals.
Entrance: Y Offset
translateY(16–20px)
Elements enter from 16–20px below their final position. Never from above. Descent signals hierarchy; ascent signals arrival.
Gold Rule Animation
scaleX(0 → 1)
Gold divider lines draw left-to-right on section entry. 680ms duration, 180ms delay from panel activation. transform-origin: left.
Chart Line Draw
stroke-dashoffset
SVG polylines animate via stroke-dashoffset → 0 on scroll enter. 1900ms duration for a deliberate, confident reveal.
Hover

Hover the pill above. The border and text transition to gold in 500ms on the IAM cubic-bezier. No snap. No spring.

05 · What IAM Never Does

Voice & Visual Prohibitions

The brand voice is institutional, precise, and cold. The visual execution is minimal and high-contrast. Certain words and treatments are explicitly prohibited — not as a stylistic preference, but as a statement of positioning.

Forbidden Words
  • "Influencer" — talent is a founder, not a vendor
  • "Synergy" — the language of consultants, not operators
  • "Going viral" — attention is not a strategy
  • "Exciting opportunity" — we arrive, we do not excite
  • "Passionate about" — conviction needs no modifier
  • "Leverage" — as a verb; it is imprecise
IAM Voice Patterns
  • "We structure ownership." — simple declarative
  • "We arrive with a term sheet." — action, not aspiration
  • "Talent is a founder, not a vendor." — reframe, not pitch
  • "We model before we approach." — method, not mystery
  • "Equity over commission." — values as structure
  • "Legacy over transaction." — time horizon stated plainly
Forbidden Visual Treatments
  • Rotating or stretching the IAM wordmark
  • Placing the logo on mid-tone or patterned backgrounds
  • Using gold (#B8A882) as a background or fill color
  • Entrance animations with scale(), rotate(), bounce
  • Any font substitution for the four specified typefaces
  • Montserrat at weight 400+ for labels/captions
Correct Visual Treatments
  • Logo on black (#0A0A0A) or white (#F5F5F0) only
  • Gold used only for DM Mono data, gold rules, apex mark
  • Entrance: opacity + translateY only — always from below
  • Section labels: Montserrat 200, 10px, 0.22em tracking
  • Numerical data in DM Mono exclusively — no exceptions
  • Minimum clear space: 1× "I" letterform height on all sides
06 · Brand Philosophy

Three Principles

Structure over Campaign
Campaigns end. Structure compounds. IAM builds operating entities, not marketing moments.
Equity over Commission
Commission belongs to the service economy. Equity belongs to the founder economy. IAM operates in the latter.
Legacy over Transaction
A deal closes. A company grows. IAM structures for the ten-year outcome, not the immediate return.
