Intellectual Capital
Is the New Asset Class.

IAM structures co-ownership vehicles, loyalty equity, and long-horizon brand equity for the most culturally significant talent of this generation. We arrive with a term sheet, not a pitch.

01

The Premise

The existing talent management model was designed for a different era. It optimizes for short-term income — campaign fees, appearance deals, licensing arrangements — and treats talent as a service provider rather than a capital-generating asset.

IAM was founded on a different premise: the most commercially significant talent of this generation carries a form of intellectual capital that, properly structured, functions identically to a recurring revenue business. The trust an audience places in a talent is not attention — it is loyalty. And loyalty, when product-matched correctly, is the foundation of a subscription company.

"The most valuable thing talent owns is not their talent. It is the trust their audience places in them."

We structure that trust into ownership. We do not campaign. We do not endorse. We build businesses alongside talent who understand the difference between being paid for their time and being paid for what they built.

02

The Structural Thesis

Subscription-native, audience-native, self-funding.

IAM operates within one thesis: talent with deep audience trust, positioned correctly, can launch subscription-native consumer businesses that fund their own growth. No external capital required at scale. No dilutive round. No brand dependency.

The mechanism is straightforward. An engaged audience — one that has elected to follow a talent across platforms, purchased prior merchandise, attended live events — already exhibits subscription behavior. They pay for proximity. IAM formalizes that behavior into a product architecture designed for long-horizon retention.

All three scenarios share the same structural logic. The growth driver changes — the operating model does not.

Each scenario begins with an organic launch phase — zero paid acquisition — followed by structured paid acceleration once organic performance validates the product. Subscriber retention compounds from month six onward.

04

Talent Compensation

We pay talent to start. We pay talent as it grows. We pay talent when it exits.

IAM structures three discrete compensation layers into every operating agreement. These are not promises — they are contractual obligations, defined at execution, before the business generates its first dollar.

1 ·
Upfront Signing Payment
Talent receives a fixed cash payment at execution of the operating agreement. The amount is calibrated to audience scale, category, and projected retention. This is not an advance — it is compensation for the commercial value of the talent's participation, paid before the product launches.
2 ·
Personal Milestone Bonuses
Revenue thresholds written into the operating agreement trigger structured cash distributions to the talent principal. Milestones are set at Month 3, Month 12, and Month 24. Growth is shared directly with the person who made it possible, on a schedule that cannot be renegotiated.
3 ·
Equity in the Operating LLC
Talent holds an equity position in the operating entity from day one. The entity is structured for participation in any exit event — acquisition, recapitalization, or strategic sale. Talent owns a piece of what they built. It is in the agreement, not in the pitch deck.

How IAM Works

IAM does not manage talent in the traditional sense. We identify talent with structural commercial potential, model the business case before making contact, and approach with a built operating framework. We do not pitch. We propose.

Every engagement begins with a proprietary audience analysis: follower composition, engagement quality, existing commercial behavior, product category match. We do not pursue talent with large audiences. We pursue talent with loyal ones.

From there, IAM builds the operating entity, sources the product infrastructure, establishes the subscription architecture, and manages the growth mechanics from organic launch through paid acceleration. We are operators, not consultants. We hold equity. We have skin in the outcome.

"We arrive with a term sheet, not a pitch. That is the difference between a partner and a vendor."

Once the business is operational, IAM manages the ongoing growth mechanics — content calendar alignment, retention programming, milestone tracking, and exit preparation. Our involvement does not end at launch. It scales with the business.
